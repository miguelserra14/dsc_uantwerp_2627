% --- 3.1 Pipeline and leakage ---------------------------------------
\paragraph{Pipeline and leakage control.}
All transformations are fitted on training folds only and applied to
validation, test and scoring data; the preprocessor is part of a
scikit-learn \citep{pedregosa2011scikit} \texttt{Pipeline} and is refitted in every cross-validation
fold, so target-based encodings never see the held-out labels.
